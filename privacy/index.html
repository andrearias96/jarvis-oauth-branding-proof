Privacy

Jarvis / Life OS is an owner-only personal productivity tool. If the owner authorizes Google Calendar access, Jarvis reads calendar lists and events with read-only permission for agenda, context, and prioritization. It cannot create, modify, or delete Calendar events through that credential.

Calendar data is processed and stored privately for the owner. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared commercially. This website uses no analytics, cookies, or tracking.

The owner can revoke access at any time in Google Account permissions. For deletion or revocation requests, use the app support contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
